# Research ethics

1. Credit follows the claim.
2. A transcription or linguistic annotation is not source-free merely because Linear B is deciphered.
3. Observation and known linguistic interpretation are separate layers.
4. Blind experiments must not expose the gold layer before predictions are frozen.
5. Competing scholarly analyses remain attributable assertions.
6. Digital copies sharing an upstream edition are not independent epigraphic witnesses.
7. Rights and licenses travel with derived records.
8. Missing evidence remains missing; do not fill it for schema convenience.
9. Corrections are versioned and auditable.
10. Linear B knowledge must not be projected into Linear A or Cypro-Minoan as ground truth.
