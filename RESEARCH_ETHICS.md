# Research ethics

1. Credit follows the claim.
2. A Linear B reading or linguistic annotation is not source-free merely because the script is deciphered.
3. Observation and known interpretation are separate layers.
4. Blind experiments may not expose gold before predictions are frozen.
5. Competing scholarly analyses remain attributable assertions.
6. Digital copies sharing an upstream edition are not independent epigraphic witnesses.
7. Rights and licenses travel with derived records.
8. Missing evidence remains missing.
9. Corrections are versioned and auditable.
10. Linear B knowledge must not be projected into Linear A, Cypro-Minoan or Cretan Hieroglyphic as ground truth.
11. Successful calibration validates only the tested method under tested conditions.
12. Null and failed calibrations are publishable results.
