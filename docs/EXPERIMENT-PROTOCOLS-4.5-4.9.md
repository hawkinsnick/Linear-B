# Frozen experiment protocols — 4.5 through 4.9

The repository records the protocol maturity reached while data transport remains blocked:

- **4.5** blind known-answer harness contract: observation-only input, deny gold, freeze predictions.
- **4.6** scoring contract: predeclared targets/denominators, uncertainty, null/failure reporting.
- **4.7** degradation contract: corpus loss, damage, uncertainty, fragmentation, hapax inflation and contextual concentration.
- **4.8** multi-witness contract: source lineage and independence classes prevent pseudo-replication.
- **4.9** hostile-audit contract: leakage, integrity, lineage, rights, coverage, clustering and scoring checks.

These specifications are frozen but **unexecuted**. The project therefore does not claim 4.5 blind-run success, 4.6 calibration success, 4.7 degradation results, 4.8 populated multi-witness coverage or a passed 4.9 hostile audit.

## 5.0 hard gate
5.0 requires: independently verified local corpus bytes; populated observation and gold layers; passing leakage audit; frozen blind predictions before reveal; executed scoring; executed degradation controls; and no unresolved critical hostile-audit failure.
