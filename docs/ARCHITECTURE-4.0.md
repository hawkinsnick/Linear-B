# Architecture — 4.0.0

## Evidence
Detector-visible records only: document/object identity, physical locus, sign occurrence, source-defined sequence structure, layout, damage, uncertainty, support, site, chronology and hand where sourced.

## Witnesses
Every transcription, restoration, identification, dating and contextual statement is an assertion with source, locator, lineage and uncertainty. Competing responsible assertions may coexist.

## Gold
Known linguistic interpretation is explicit and separately exportable: normalized readings, lexical forms, lemmas, morphology, syntax, semantics and higher interpretation. Gold is never silently reachable through observation adapters.

## Research
Blind experiments consume observation-only exports. Predictions freeze before gold reveal.

## Interchange
Aegean Epigraphy Interchange v0.1 includes only script-neutral abstractions. Linear-B-specific linguistic fields remain extensions.

## Clients
Search, API, CLI and export clients cannot redefine evidence.

## Dependency rule
Interpretation may depend on observation; observation may not depend on interpretation.

## Non-goals
4.0 does not imply comprehensive coverage, successful calibration, universal agreement on readings, or permission to redistribute protected editions/images.
