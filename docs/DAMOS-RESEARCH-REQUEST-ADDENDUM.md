# DĀMOS annotated-export request addendum

For the frozen Linear B known-answer calibration, the preferred artifact is a documented or maintainer-provided structured/EpiDoc export of the linguistic annotation layer. The export should retain stable DĀMOS document/unit identifiers; morphology and syntax; lexical/semantic or translation fields where available; competing analyses and their uncertainty/probability rather than flattening them; and enough provenance to cite the source correctly.

A transliteration-only export is useful but does not satisfy the linguistic-gold gate. It also does not satisfy the separate graphical-sign baseline required for LB-RAW/LB-STRUCTURAL/LB-CONTEXT.

No undocumented/private endpoint access is permitted. No linguistic gold may be generated by the project itself.
