# KN Fp 13 museum and source concordance

The [British Museum catalogue](https://www.britishmuseum.org/collection/object/G_1910-0423-1) now loads through ordinary HTTP. It identifies clay tablet **1910,0423.1** with the edition reference **CoMIK I, Fp(1) 13**, p. 9, and reports the findspot Knossos, Room of the Clay Chest. The [Cambridge MAA catalogue](https://collections.maa.cam.ac.uk/objects/600876/) explicitly identifies **Z 46404 B** as a modern plaster cast of KN Fp 13 and names the original British Museum registration.

The authenticated DĀMOS source record 13 has the exact heading `KN Fp(1) 13` and inventory `BM (GR1910.4-23.1)`. The concordance therefore rests on edition labels and explicit inventory assertions, not a guessed accession or numeric-ID-only match. Source URLs, response hashes, exact source fields and the replica relationship are recorded in `research/kn-fp13-object-concordance.json`. The original frozen DĀMOS fields are unchanged.

This closes the bounded access/concordance task **LB-BM** for one document. It does not certify graphical occurrences or close the linguistic calibration gate. Cambridge's cast is a reproduction, not a second ancient inscription or an independent primary glyph witness. Its historical date fields must not be assigned as the manufacture date of the cast. The British Museum's circa 1375 BCE assertion and DĀMOS's `LM IIIA2 or LM IIIB` assertion remain separately attributed; no chronology is adjudicated by normalization.

Only project-authored factual catalogue assertions are added. No museum image or protected descriptive text is redistributed, and no image/benchmark permission is inferred. Public reuse of contributed images or a systematic graphical package remains a separate rights question.
