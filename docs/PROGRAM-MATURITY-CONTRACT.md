# Aegean Epigraphy Program Maturity Contract v1

Repository version numbers are local semantic versions, not cross-project rankings.

Every project reports six dimensions independently: architecture; authenticated evidence; source-checked coverage; executed analysis; external/control validation; rights and provenance.

Readiness is question-specific. Architecture cannot substitute for evidence coverage; catalogue completeness cannot substitute for occurrence verification; structural results are not decipherment; and deciphered controls may not redefine the neutral interchange core.

Shared invariants: credit follows claims and lineage; digital re-encoding does not create independent epigraphic evidence; competing responsible assertions may coexist; rights travel with derivatives; script identity, sign identity, phonetic value and linguistic interpretation remain distinct; blocked experiments permit no claims; null/negative results are preserved; cross-script algorithms consume neutral interchange rather than assumed native equivalences.
